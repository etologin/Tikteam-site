How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your LIVE

    Enter your TikTok username in the app. Gifts, likes, follows and shares reach TikTeam as soon as your LIVE starts.

  2. 2

    Set the rules

    Pick a gift from the catalogue and assign an action: spawn a fighter, heal, buff, play a clip. You choose the count, damage, team and duration.

  3. 3

    Play together

    Viewers see themselves on screen: their name and avatar appear right in the game or in the clip. Queues and limits keep the stream under control, even when a hundred gifts arrive at once.

Modules

Avatar Overlay

A gift plays a video with the viewer’s avatar and name. In LIVE Studio, capture the TikTeam overlays window.

Battle Overlay

Battle effects play in response to TikTok events or a key you assign.

TikDonate

Android / iPhone

Donations via Kaspi / Halyk / Freedom transfers and DonationAlerts: on-stream alerts, top donors and a donation goal.

Dead as Disco

Viewers enter the arena as enemies, allies or bosses, with their name and avatar overhead. The mod installs from the app, and the game files it changes are backed up first.

Streets of Rage 4

A gift spawns a fighter carrying the viewer's name; health and team depend on the gift. Blue vs red team battles with rounds and overtime.
