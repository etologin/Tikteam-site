How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your LIVE

    Enter your TikTok username in the app. Gifts, likes, follows and shares reach TikTeam as soon as your LIVE starts.

  2. 2

    Set the rules

    Pick a gift from the catalogue and assign an action: spawn a fighter, heal, buff, play a clip. You choose the count, damage, team and duration.

  3. 3

    Play together

    Viewers see themselves on screen: their name and avatar appear right in the game or in the clip. Queues and limits keep the stream under control, even when a hundred gifts arrive at once.

Features

  • Gift catalogue

    Images and coin prices, search by name. Rules are tied to a specific gift, not to a random word in chat.

  • More than gifts

    Separate rules for likes, follows, subscriptions and shares.

  • Queues and limits

    Large gifts are split into portions and donors are served in turn, so one viewer can't take over the whole stream.

  • Names in the game

    The viewer's name, avatar and health are shown right inside the game frame.

  • Action sounds

    Your own sound for every action — from a file or from MyInstants.

  • Careful installs

    Before installing a mod, the app saves a copy of the game files it changes.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit)
  • A TikTok account with LIVE access
  • Your own legal copy of the game — for game modules

TikTeam does not distribute games or game files. Mods are installed into your own copy of the game.

FAQ

How much does it cost?

Each module is a separate subscription. Prices will be announced before the public launch.

Does it work on Mac or mobile?

No. TikTeam is a Windows 10 and 11 app.

Do I need to buy the game?

Yes, game modules need your own copy of the game. Avatar Overlay and TikTok Overlay don't require a game.

When can I try it?

We're in closed beta. Join our Discord for news and beta invites.
