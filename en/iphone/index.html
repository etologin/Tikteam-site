Kaspi on iPhone

Connect Kaspi Gold top-up notifications to TikDonate.

Get a key or QR code in your account

Setup

  1. On your computer, open TikDonate → Connections → Connect Kaspi from iPhone.
  2. Select Connect iPhone and scan the QR code with your iPhone camera within five minutes.
  3. Select Copy code and open Shortcuts. Add the shortcut and paste the code during setup.
  4. Enable the Kaspi.kz automation. Open Shortcuts → Automation, select the automation for Kaspi.kz notifications, and turn it on. Choose Run Immediately if that option is available.
  5. Keep TikDonate running. After the next Kaspi Gold top-up notification, check the amount and sender in your donation history.

If you already copied your key, you can open the shortcut directly.

Open iCloud shortcut

The key stays valid until you unlink it. Each account has one iPhone connection. To switch phones, select Unlink iPhone in TikDonate.
