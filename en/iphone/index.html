Connect iPhone in your account

Setup

  1. Open your TikTeam account and select Connect iPhone.
  2. Scan the QR code within five minutes, or copy the key shown below it and open the shortcut on your iPhone.
  3. Paste the key when adding the shortcut.
  4. Enable the automation for Kaspi.kz, Freedom and Halyk. Remove the shared Kaspi Gold title filter and select Run Immediately if available.

If you already copied your key, you can open the shortcut directly.

Open iCloud shortcut

The key stays valid until you unlink it. Each account has one iPhone connection. To switch phones, select Unlink iPhone in TikDonate.
